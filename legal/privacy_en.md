# Privacy Policy — Fantasy Color

**Last updated:** 29 September 2026  
**Operator:** Schwaben Apps  
**Contact:** fantasycolor@schwabenapps.com  
**App:** Fantasy Color (package / bundle ID: `com.schwabenapps.mkz.fantasyColor`)

This Privacy Policy explains how Schwaben Apps (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use Fantasy Color on iOS or Android.

Fantasy Color is designed for children and families. We aim to collect as little data as possible.

---

## 1. Who we are

Schwaben Apps operates Fantasy Color.  
For privacy questions, contact us at **fantasycolor@schwabenapps.com**.

---

## 2. What the app stores on your device

The following stays **on your device** and is not uploaded to Schwaben Apps as personal content:

- Coloring and puzzle progress
- Favorites
- App settings (for example music on/off)
- Locally saved or exported images you create (when you choose to save/share)

We do **not** require an account to use Fantasy Color.

---

## 3. Information we process

### 3.1 Anonymous analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (for example which screens are opened). This helps us improve Fantasy Color.

Analytics are configured without collecting names, email addresses, or personal profiles of children. Events are used in aggregate form.

### 3.2 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, we may receive technical crash diagnostics (for example device model, OS version, and stack traces) so we can fix bugs. These reports are intended for stability, not for identifying a child.

### 3.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

Fantasy Color may show ads. Ads are configured for **child-directed / age-restricted treatment** and **G-rated** ad content where supported by the ad SDK and platform rules.

Ad providers may process device and advertising identifiers according to their own policies and applicable law (including parental/guardian consent requirements where required).

### 3.4 Online content packs

The app may download additional image packs from our content delivery network (for example seasonal event images). Downloads are app content, not personal messages from users.

### 3.5 Support and feedback

If you contact us by email or send feedback through your mail app, we receive the content you send (and your email address if you include one) so we can respond. We use this only for support.

---

## 4. What we do not do

- We do **not** sell your personal data.
- We do **not** ask children to provide a name, photo, or contact details to use the core app.
- We do **not** use your drawings as a cloud social feed.

---

## 5. Children / kids-directed use

Fantasy Color is intended for children and families. We design data practices with child privacy in mind (COPPA-oriented / child-directed ad settings where applicable).

Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising device use and reviewing platform privacy settings (Apple / Google).

---

## 6. Sharing of information

We use service providers that process technical data on our behalf, including:

- **Google Firebase** (Analytics, Crashlytics)
- **Google AdMob** (ads)
- **Cloudflare** (hosting / delivery of image packs; email routing for support)

These providers process data under their terms and privacy policies. We do not sell data to third parties.

---

## 7. Data retention

- On-device progress/favorites remain until you delete the app or clear app data.
- Analytics and crash data are retained according to Google Firebase retention settings.
- Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

---

## 8. Your choices

- Turn music on/off in Settings.
- Limit ad tracking / personalized ads in your device settings (where available).
- Uninstall the app to remove local app data from the device.
- Contact us to ask questions about this policy: **fantasycolor@schwabenapps.com**

Platform tools (Apple / Google) may also provide additional privacy controls.

Our service providers may process data on servers outside your country (including the EU and the United States), subject to applicable safeguards and provider terms.
